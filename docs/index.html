Installation & macOS Security

Your download has started from GitHub Releases. Because Cafaine is free and open-source without an Apple Developer ID signature, follow these simple steps on first launch:

Cafaine.dmg v1.0.0
Native Apple Disk Image (Universal for Apple Silicon & Intel)
Download DMG
1
Mount and Drag to Applications
Open Cafaine.dmg and drag Cafaine.app into the Applications shortcut. (This installs it and indexes it in Launchpad).
2
macOS Gatekeeper Permission
If macOS displays "Cannot verify developer":
1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
2. Scroll down to Security.
3. Click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password or Touch ID. Or Terminal: xattr -cr /Applications/Cafaine.app
3
Look at your Menu Bar
Cafaine lives strictly in the top Menu Bar as a coffee cup icon. It has no dock clutter. Click the cup or press Option + C to activate!
