Free & Open Source Native macOS Utility

Keep Your Mac Awake.
Zero Cost. Zero Clutter.

Cafaine prevents your Mac from sleeping, dimming the screen, or starting screensavers. Built with native Apple IOKit assertions, clamshell lid-closed support, and zero background overhead.
